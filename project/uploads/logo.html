01. The Concept

The White Swan,
Translated into Geometry.

To visualize the "White Swan" principle, we avoided literal interpretations of birds or generic camera icons. Instead, we focused on the core action: Viewing and Bookmarking.

The logo is constructed from two precise geometric strokes forming a "V". The left stroke is solid and grounded, representing your curated media collection. The right stroke is translucent and fades away, representing the invisible, friction-less design interface. Together, they subtly form a downward arrow (bookmarking) and a perspective play button.
